Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 1, 2026

What Stocheo accesses

The Stocheo Google Sheets™ add-on operates entirely within your current spreadsheet. It reads cell values you pass to its functions and writes results to the same spreadsheet. It does not access any other files in your Google account.

Data collection

Stocheo does not collect, transmit, or store any of your spreadsheet data. All computation happens locally within Google Sheets. No data is sent to external servers.

Cookies and tracking

This website does not use cookies or analytics tracking.

Third-party services

Stocheo does not share data with any third parties. The add-on has no external network dependencies.

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new effective date.
